Keep a restorable backup

Open Settings and export JSON. Use the device share sheet to save it outside the app, then verify the destination file. Opening or closing the share sheet does not prove a backup was saved. CSV is for spreadsheets, not restore.

Restore a notebook

Select a JSON backup in Settings. Review the preview, then confirm replacement only if you want to discard the current notebook. Cancel leaves the notebook unchanged. A backup must use the supported schema and be no larger than 5 MiB. A compact input can still be rejected if its canonical exported backup would exceed the limit.

If an older notebook is already larger than 5 MiB, it remains readable and exportable, but an edit or deletion can be saved only when that SINGLE change produces a complete backup at or below 5 MiB. Deletions that still leave it oversized are not saved and cannot accumulate. First export and verify the original outside the app. Keep that original safe; prepare a smaller schema-compatible JSON copy externally, then preview and explicitly replace with that copy. Do not reset or overwrite your only original backup to reduce size. CoopCost does not provide a batch-reduction tool.

If storage cannot be opened

Do not reset first. Try reopening/retry. When raw saved text is available, export it before considering reset. Future-version or malformed data is not silently erased. Reset is destructive and requires confirmation. Keep your original backups; support cannot promise recovery.

If a write has an uncertain outcome

A storage error can occur even after a write committed. CoopCost reads storage back before permitting another edit. If the previous saved data is verified unchanged, your draft remains available for retry. Otherwise editing and normal JSON/CSV snapshot exports are blocked so the old view cannot overwrite or masquerade as the saved notebook.

Use Retry storage check — keep drafts, or export the original saved text using Export raw recovery data. Discard drafts and reopen saved data explicitly abandons unsaved forms and reads storage again without writing. If the saved text cannot be opened, Discard drafts and open recovery enables the normal recovery flow; deleting storage still requires its separate two-stage confirmation. A share sheet closing never confirms the destination saved a file.

Private export copies are retained beyond share completion; a later export attempts expired-only cleanup after a 24-hour grace period. Copies can persist longer, and the OS can evict them earlier. At the 20-entry export-cache cap, another export fails instead of deleting recent copies. Keep and verify an external backup; cached copies are not a backup guarantee.

Understanding the summary

Monthly operating expense is feed plus other operating purchases; setup purchases are shown separately. Bulk purchases belong to their purchase month. The per-egg figure divides recorded operating purchases by recorded eggs. It is not profit, actual feed consumed or an estimate of missing records. Zero recorded eggs means no per-egg figure.

Currency and edits

Choose one supported currency at setup. While any records exist the currency is locked to avoid reinterpreting existing amounts. Flock name can be edited. No exchange-rate conversion is performed.

CoopCost Pro, purchases and refunds (version 1.1 and later)

What does CoopCost Pro add? Summaries for months other than the current month, annual totals and CSV export. Pro is one optional one-time in-app purchase; there is no subscription and nothing renews automatically.

What stays free? Egg and expense logging, the current-month summary, and JSON backup and restore. (Each notebook holds up to 20,000 egg days and 20,000 expenses, within a 5 MiB backup.)

How much does Pro cost? The price is set in Google Play and shown in the app before purchase.

How do I get Pro back after reinstalling the app or using a new phone? Install the app, sign in with the same Google account, then use Settings → Unlock Pro → Restore purchases. JSON backups never grant or remove Pro.

Who handles refunds? Google Play handles refunds, not the developer. After a refund is granted, the app removes Pro at its next successful check.

Does buying Pro send the notebook anywhere? No. Google Play tells the app only whether Pro is owned; the app keeps just a yes/no unlock flag and the time it was last checked on your device. Flock records are never sent to Google Play or the developer, and CoopCost never sees payment-card details.

Reports

When a monitored support channel is available, include app version, device/OS, what you tried and the exact error. Do not send payment-card information, passwords or private notebook contents. App-store download/refund questions go to the relevant store.
