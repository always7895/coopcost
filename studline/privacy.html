StudLine Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-11

Privacy Policy Support Contact: moon951753@gmail.com

What is stored on your device

StudLine stores your app settings, your running tape of measurements and your saved jobs (including the notes you type into them) on your device. The app has no account and no developer-operated backend, and it contains no ads and no analytics. Calculations are arithmetic performed on the device.

No data is sent to the developer

No data is collected and sent to the developer. The app stores everything locally, and the release build's merged Android manifest does not request the INTERNET permission, so the app itself cannot send your data anywhere.

Permissions

The release build's merged manifest contains com.android.vending.BILLING and android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (added by Google Play Billing and its bundled Google libraries for the optional purchase) and an internal signature-level receiver permission added by AndroidX. The app does not request INTERNET, storage, exact-alarm, location, camera or contacts permissions.

Exports leave the app only when you send them

You can export a JSON backup, restore one, export CSV, and share a mark list or a saved job as text. These are user-initiated actions: nothing leaves the app unless you choose to export or share it, and the recipient or app you choose may then store or forward what you sent according to its own terms. StudLine does not receive a copy of what you export and cannot confirm that a share was saved by the recipient. Share only with recipients you trust.

App data sits in the app's own private storage on the device; that is not an additional encryption guarantee. Operating-system backup, device transfer and third-party file providers may copy data depending on device and service settings. The app allows Android backup (android:allowBackup is true), so Android's own device backup may include StudLine's data in your Google account backup when you have device backup turned on. The developer cannot access that backup.

Purchases

StudLine is free to use, and StudLine Pro is one optional one-time in-app purchase. It is not a subscription, there is no trial, and no card is needed to use the free calculator. Purchases are handled by Google Play under Google's terms; the price is set in Google Play and shown in the app before purchase. The developer does not process the payment and does not receive your payment-card details. After reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone, use Restore purchases with the same Google account. JSON backups never grant or remove Pro. Refunds are handled by Google Play. Pro is available on Android through Google Play only.

Retention and deletion

Records have no scheduled expiry: your tape and jobs stay on the device until you delete them. You can delete your own entries inside the app, and uninstalling removes the app's local data. Uninstall and backup behaviour depend on the operating system and its settings. Deleting local data does not delete files you already shared or exported, recipient copies or operating-system backups; manage those with the relevant provider. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, the developer cannot recover a lost tape or job list, and there is no developer-side deletion request to make.

If you contact support

Support e-mail: moon951753@gmail.com. If you contact support, send only what is needed to describe the problem; do not send a backup file or your job list unless it is necessary and both sides agree to it.

Accuracy of results

Every result in StudLine is arithmetic only, and the app makes no building-code, structural or engineering claims. Check measurements on site before cutting.
