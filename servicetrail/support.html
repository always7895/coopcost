Keep a restorable backup

Open Settings and export a JSON backup. Use the device share sheet to save it somewhere you control, then check the saved file. Closing the share sheet does not guarantee that a file was saved. CSV export (one row per service item and per fill-up) is for spreadsheets and cannot be used to restore data.

Restore a backup

In Settings, choose a UTF-8 ServiceTrail JSON backup of up to 5 MiB. You will see a preview of the vehicles, service visits, fill-ups and reminder rules before anything changes. Restore replaces all current records, vehicles, currency and units; it never merges data or converts amounts. Canceling discards only the preview. If saving fails, your current records and the preview remain available.

If an import fails, keep the original file. Use a full ServiceTrail JSON backup with the supported schema and a known file size. If the file provider cannot supply the file size, try a locally downloaded copy.

If saved data cannot be opened

If ServiceTrail cannot open its saved data, it shows a recovery screen. The data may be damaged, may come from a newer version of the app, or may be temporarily unavailable on this device. The app does not automatically reset or overwrite it.

Do not reset first. Check that the device has free storage, then use Retry loading saved data. If the data came from a newer ServiceTrail version, keep the original export and use a compatible app version instead of resetting. You can use Export original data to share the exact saved text without repairing it; damaged or newer-version data may not be importable. Reset permanently deletes all on-device vehicles, service records, reminders and fuel-ups, requires two confirmation steps, and has no undo or cloud backup. Previously exported files are not deleted. Support cannot promise recovery.

What is free and what is Pro

Free: up to 2 active vehicles or machines (archived ones do not count, and equipment counts the same as a vehicle); service visits with parts and labor costs, part numbers, shop and notes; history grouped by service type; reminders by date, distance or both; a Due list; a fuel log; JSON backup and restore; and CSV export.

Pro (one optional one-time purchase): unlimited vehicles and machines, a service-history PDF that can also be shared as text, and yearly cost and fuel-economy summaries.

Purchases, restore and refunds

ServiceTrail Pro is one optional one-time in-app purchase. It is not a subscription, there is no trial, and nothing renews automatically. Purchases are handled by Google Play under Google's terms. The price is set in Google Play and shown in the app before you buy.

After reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone, open the app and use Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account that made the purchase. JSON backups never grant or remove Pro.

Refunds are handled by Google Play. If Pro is refunded, every vehicle, service, fill-up and reminder stays viewable, editable and exportable; only the Pro features lock again (more than 2 active vehicles or machines, the service-history PDF and text sharing, and the yearly summaries).

Reminders and notifications

Reminders can be set by date, by distance or by both. Each rule sends at most an early warning and one due-date notification, never a daily repeat, and you can snooze for 1 day, 3 days or 1 week. Android asks for notification permission when you switch reminders on. If you denied it, enable notifications for ServiceTrail in Android Settings and switch reminders on again.

Reminders are scheduled on your phone only and use inexact scheduling, so Android may deliver one a little late. A distance-only reminder estimates its date from your own odometer or hour-meter readings and is marked "estimated"; with too few readings it appears in the Due list only and says so. Scheduled reminders are restored after the phone restarts.

Reporting a problem

E-mail moon951753@gmail.com. Please include your Android version, the phone model, the ServiceTrail version, what you did, and what you expected to happen. If the problem concerns a reminder, say whether notifications are allowed for the app and how the rule is set up.

Never send payment-card or other payment details. Send only what is needed to describe the problem, and do not attach a backup file or your saved records unless it is necessary.
