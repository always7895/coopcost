Keep a restorable backup

Open Settings and export a JSON backup. Use the device share sheet to save it somewhere you control, then check the saved file. Closing the share sheet does not guarantee that a file was saved. CSV export (one row per done or skipped job and one per points entry) is for spreadsheets and cannot be used to restore data.

Restore a backup

In Settings, choose a UTF-8 TidyTurns JSON backup of up to 5 MiB. You will see a preview of the household, people, rooms, jobs and history before anything changes. Restore replaces all current records and household settings; it never merges data. Canceling discards only the preview. If saving fails, your current records and the preview remain available.

If an import fails, keep the original file. Use a full TidyTurns JSON backup with the supported schema and a known file size. If the file provider cannot supply the file size, try a locally downloaded copy.

If saved data cannot be opened

If TidyTurns cannot open its saved data, it shows a recovery screen. The data may be damaged, may come from a newer version of the app, or may be temporarily unavailable on this device. The app does not automatically reset or overwrite it.

Do not reset first. Check that the device has free storage, then use Retry loading saved data. If the data came from a newer TidyTurns version, keep the original export and use a compatible app version instead of resetting. You can use Export original data to share the exact saved text without repairing it; damaged or newer-version data may not be importable. Reset permanently deletes all on-device household settings, rooms, jobs and history, requires two confirmation steps, and has no undo or cloud backup. Previously exported files are not deleted. Support cannot promise recovery.

What is free and what is Pro

Free: unlimited rooms, jobs and people; repeat rules (every few days or weeks, set weekdays, once a month, only in some months, or a set number of days after the job was last done); taking turns between people; Today and This week views that can be filtered by person; marking a job done or skipping it; fixed weekly rest days; the optional daily summary; JSON backup and restore; and CSV export.

Pro (one optional one-time purchase): away mode, the balanced week, points per person with history, and a printable weekly plan that can be saved as a PDF or shared as text.

Purchases, restore and refunds

TidyTurns Pro is one optional one-time in-app purchase. It is not a subscription, there is no trial, and nothing renews automatically. Purchases are handled by Google Play under Google's terms. The price is set in Google Play and shown in the app before you buy.

After reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone, open the app and use Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account that made the purchase. JSON backups never grant or remove Pro.

Refunds are handled by Google Play. Nothing you have already saved is hidden when Pro is refunded.

Daily summary notification

The daily summary is one notification a day, at the time you choose, and is off by default. Turning it on asks Android for notification permission once. If notifications are off for TidyTurns, turn them on in Android Settings > Apps > TidyTurns > Notifications, then switch the summary on again.

The summary is scheduled on the phone itself and Android may deliver it a little late, depending on the device and its battery settings. Depending on your lock-screen settings, it may be visible on the lock screen. Switching the summary off removes the scheduled notification.

E-mail moon951753@gmail.com. Please include your Android version, the phone model, the TidyTurns version, what you did, and what you expected to happen. If the problem concerns the daily summary, say whether notifications are allowed for the app.

Never send payment-card or other payment details. Send only what is needed to describe the problem, and do not attach a backup file or your saved records unless it is necessary.
