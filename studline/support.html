Keep a restorable backup

Open Settings and export a JSON backup. Use the device share sheet to save it somewhere you control, then check the saved file. Closing the share sheet does not guarantee that a file was saved, and the app cannot tell whether you saved, shared or canceled. CSV export is for spreadsheets and cannot be used to restore data.

Restore a backup

In Settings, choose a UTF-8 StudLine JSON backup of up to 5 MiB. You will see a preview before anything changes. Restore replaces all current jobs, tape entries and settings; it never merges data, and there is no undo after a successful replacement, so export your current backup first if you might need it. Canceling discards only the preview. If saving fails, your current data and the preview remain available.

If an import fails, keep the original file. Use a full StudLine JSON backup with the supported schema and a known file size. If the file provider cannot supply the file size, try a locally downloaded copy.

If saved data cannot be opened

If StudLine cannot open its saved data, it shows a recovery screen. The data may be damaged, may come from a newer version of the app, or may be temporarily unavailable on this device. The app does not automatically reset or overwrite it.

Do not reset first. Check that the device has free storage, then use Retry loading saved data. If the data came from a newer StudLine version, keep the original export and use a compatible app version instead of resetting. You can use Export original data to share the exact saved text without repairing it; damaged or newer-version data may not be importable. Reset permanently deletes all on-device settings, tape history and saved jobs, requires two confirmation steps, and has no undo or cloud backup. Previously exported files are not deleted. Support cannot promise recovery.

If a change might not have been saved

If saving fails, do not assume your change was stored. Stay on the form, keep your input, and retry after addressing the device or storage error shown. If sharing is unavailable, no export has been confirmed.

What is free and what is Pro

Free: the calculator with a running tape that is kept when you close and reopen the app (up to 200 lines), unit-aware math, fractions down to 1/64 with the rounding difference shown, conversions, triangle tools, area, volume and board feet with a waste percentage, on-center layout with a mark list, equal-part division, JSON backup and restore, and CSV export.

Pro (one optional one-time purchase): stairs with a stringer mark list, rafters, raked wall stud heights, and saved jobs with notes and text sharing.

Purchases, restore and refunds

StudLine Pro is one optional one-time in-app purchase. It is not a subscription, there is no trial, and nothing renews automatically. Purchases are handled by Google Play under Google's terms. The price is set in Google Play and shown in the app before you buy.

After reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone, open the app and use Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account that made the purchase. JSON backups never grant or remove Pro.

Refunds are handled by Google Play. Google Play is the source of purchase status.

Reporting a problem

E-mail moon951753@gmail.com. Please include your Android version, the phone model, the StudLine version, what you did, and what you expected to happen. If the problem is a calculation, describe the measurements you entered.

Never send payment-card or other payment details. Send only what is needed to describe the problem, and do not attach a backup file or your saved records unless it is necessary.
