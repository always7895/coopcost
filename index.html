CoopCost

Privacy Policy Support Egg Cost Calculator Contact: moon951753@gmail.com

CoopCost keeps an egg-flock notebook on your device: it stores your flock name, selected currency, daily egg totals, expenses and notes there. Free to download, with an optional one-time Pro unlock (version 1.1 and later). The app has no account, developer-operated backend, analytics or advertising integration, and it does not request your financial account or payment-card details; optional purchases are processed by Google Play under Google's terms. CoopCost does not intentionally transmit your notebook to its developer — you choose whether to export CSV or JSON through your device's share sheet, or import a JSON backup from a document provider, and you can restore, reset and delete your own records at any time.
