TidyTurns Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-11

Privacy Policy Support Contact: moon951753@gmail.com

Who the app is for

TidyTurns is for adults aged 18 and over who share one device. There are no accounts of any kind and no profile for anyone under 18.

What is stored on your device

TidyTurns stores the following on your device, in the app's own private storage:

  • the household name;
  • the people in the household: the names you enter and their chosen colours;
  • rooms and jobs, including each job's repeat rule (every few days or weeks, set weekdays, once a month, months active, or a number of days after it was last done), who it is assigned to and whether it rotates round-robin;
  • the done and skip history of those jobs;
  • points per person and their history;
  • time away recorded in away mode;
  • settings, including the daily-summary time, rest days and the Pro entitlement state.

The app has no account and no developer-operated backend, and it contains no ads and no analytics. There is no sync between phones: everyone uses the same phone or tablet, so the schedule lives on that one device.

No data is sent to the developer

No data is collected and sent to the developer. The release build has no android.permission.INTERNET permission (per its merged Android manifest), so the app itself cannot send anything over the network. Names, jobs, history and points stay in the app's private storage on your phone and leave it only through a backup, export, print or share that you start. The notification library brings Firebase messaging components with it; they have no configuration in this app and no network access, so they send nothing. There is no crash reporting, analytics or advertising SDK.

Permissions

Permissions in the release build's merged manifest:

  • com.android.vending.BILLING, for the optional one-time purchase, and android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, added by the billing and notification libraries.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS, requested only when you switch the daily summary on, plus android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED and android.permission.WAKE_LOCK so a scheduled summary is restored after the phone restarts.
  • Nothing else: no internet, location, camera, contacts, storage or exact-alarm permission. The daily summary is one inexact notification a day.

The daily summary notification

The optional daily summary is at most one notification a day, at the time you chose, and is off by default. It is scheduled on the phone itself. Nothing is sent to the developer to trigger it, and the developer cannot see it or its contents. Because it is scheduled inexactly, Android may deliver it a little late, depending on the device and its battery settings. Switching the summary off removes the scheduled notification. The summary lists room and job names, and a person's name when a job is theirs alone; depending on your lock-screen settings, Android may show it on the lock screen.

Exports, backups and PDFs leave the app only when you share them

You can export a JSON backup, restore one, export CSV, save the weekly plan as a PDF, and share the weekly plan as text. These are all user-initiated actions: nothing leaves the app unless you choose to export, share or print it, and the recipient, printing service or app you choose may then store or forward what you sent according to its own terms. TidyTurns does not receive a copy of what you export and cannot confirm that a share was saved or printed by the recipient. Share only with recipients you trust.

App data sits in the app's own private storage on the device; that is not an additional encryption guarantee.

Operating-system backup behaviour: the app allows Android backup (android:allowBackup is true), so if device backup is turned on, Android's own backup may include TidyTurns' data in your Google account backup, and device transfer may copy it to a new phone. Third-party file providers may copy files you export or share, depending on their settings. The developer cannot access your device backup.

Purchases

TidyTurns is free to use, and TidyTurns Pro is one optional one-time in-app purchase. It is not a subscription, there is no trial countdown, and nothing renews automatically. Purchases are handled by Google Play under Google's terms; the price is set in Google Play and shown in the app before purchase. The developer does not process the payment and does not receive your payment-card details. After reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone, use Restore purchases with the same Google account. JSON backups never grant or remove Pro. Refunds are handled by Google Play, and nothing you have already saved is hidden when Pro is refunded. Pro is available on Android through Google Play only.

Retention and deletion

Records have no scheduled expiry: the household, jobs and history stay on the device until you delete them. You can delete your own rooms, jobs, people and history inside the app, and uninstalling removes the app's local data. Uninstall and backup behaviour depend on the operating system and its settings. Deleting local data does not delete files you already shared, exported or printed, recipient copies or operating-system backups; manage those with the relevant provider. Because the developer holds no copy of your data, the developer cannot recover a lost household or schedule, and there is no developer-side deletion request to make.

If you contact support

Support e-mail: moon951753@gmail.com. If you contact support, send only what is needed to describe the problem; do not send a backup file or your whole schedule unless it is necessary and both sides agree to it.

Scope

This policy is about the Android app only: one shared device, no sync between phones, one optional daily summary and one optional one-time purchase.
