What is stored on your device

CoopCost stores your flock name, selected currency, daily egg totals, expenses and notes on your device. The app has no account, developer-operated backend, analytics or advertising integration. It does not request your financial account or payment-card details. Optional purchases (version 1.1 and later) are processed by Google Play under Google's terms.

Purchases (version 1.1 and later)

CoopCost is free to download, and an optional one-time in-app purchase (CoopCost Pro) is processed by Google Play under Google's terms; the price is set in Google Play and shown in the app before purchase. CoopCost never sees your payment-card or financial-account details. Google Play tells the app only whether Pro is owned, and the app stores just a yes/no unlock flag and the time it was last checked on your device; your flock records are never sent to Google Play or to the developer. After reinstalling the app or using a new phone, use Restore purchases with the same Google account. JSON backups never grant or remove Pro. Refunds are handled by Google Play; after a refund the app removes Pro at its next successful check. Pro is available on Android through Google Play only. The app collects and shares no data; optional purchases are processed by Google Play.

Exporting, importing and sharing

CoopCost does not intentionally transmit your notebook to its developer. You can choose to export CSV or JSON through your device's share sheet, or import a JSON file from a document provider. The recipient or cloud provider you choose may receive and store that file according to its own terms. JSON backups and CSV files are not encrypted by CoopCost. Recovery exports can contain the original stored text. Share only with recipients you trust. Avoid entering sensitive personal information in notes.

App data, device backup and recovery of a lost notebook

App data is stored in the application's private storage; this is not an additional encryption guarantee. Operating-system backup, device transfer and third-party file providers may copy data depending on device and service settings. On Android, the app allows the system backup service, so Android Auto Backup and device-to-device transfer may include app data depending on your device and Google account settings; those copies are managed by Google under your account. The developer cannot recover a lost notebook. Keep your own exported backup.

Editing, restoring, resetting and deleting

You can edit/delete individual entries. Confirmed JSON restore replaces the current notebook and settings; it does not merge. The app's recovery reset removes the locally stored snapshot after explicit confirmation. Deleting local records does not delete previously shared/exported files, recipient copies or operating-system backups. Manage those through the relevant provider. Uninstall behavior depends on the operating system and backup settings.

Export files and cleanup

Exports use a dedicated app-private cache directory. Once a file may have been handed to the native share sheet, CoopCost keeps that copy even if sharing resolves, is canceled, or reports an error: those outcomes do not prove the recipient saved or finished reading it. On a later user-requested export, CoopCost attempts to delete only recognized export files whose filename and modification timestamps are both at least 24 hours old. Within the running app, it also protects outstanding shares and waits 24 hours after their completion before cleanup. There is no background or startup cleanup. Copies may remain longer if you do not export again, metadata is unknown, or cleanup fails.

Export cache limits, timing and verification

The directory is limited to 20 entries for new export creation (unknown entries also consume capacity). When it is full, a new export reports an error rather than deleting younger or uncertain copies to make room. Failed operations before any native handoff may remove their own newly created output immediately. Device clock rollback or future timestamps can delay cleanup; forward clock changes within the running app do not shorten the grace period. After an app restart, expiry relies on device/file timestamps, not a trusted elapsed-time source. The operating system can evict cache earlier at any time. This policy is not a guarantee of retention, successful delivery, or a saved backup; verify your chosen recipient's saved copy.

Import cleanup and contacting support

App-owned temporary import copies are cleaned up on a best-effort basis; cleanup failures may leave cached copies. Unknown files, directories and file providers' originals are not intentionally deleted. If you contact support, provide only the information needed; do not send a private notebook unless necessary and explicitly agreed.
