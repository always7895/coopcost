Egg Cost Calculator

Find the cost per egg and per dozen for your backyard flock. Use the same period for all three inputs, and the same currency for both costs. Enter amounts without a currency symbol.

Results update as you type. Enter 0 for any costs you did not have.

Use the formula below to calculate your egg costs.

How to calculate egg costs

Total costs = feed cost + other costs.
Cost per egg = total costs / eggs collected.
Cost per dozen = cost per egg × 12.

Include feed used during the period and ongoing costs such as bedding and supplements. Count the eggs collected during that same period. If no eggs were collected, there is no cost-per-egg figure to calculate yet.

One-time setup costs, such as a coop or equipment, are usually tracked separately from ongoing costs.

Worked example: feed costs of 40 plus other costs of 10 give total costs of 50. With 100 eggs collected, the cost per egg is 50 / 100 = 0.50, and the cost per dozen is 0.50 × 12 = 6.00, in your chosen currency. Results are rounded to two decimal places after calculation.
